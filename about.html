Our Story

A Legacy of Quality Construction

BuildPro Construction was founded in 2008 with a simple but powerful idea: construction should be done right, the first time. From a small team of three engineers in New York, we've grown into a multidisciplinary firm of over 120 professionals delivering projects across the United States.

Our portfolio spans residential, commercial, industrial and public infrastructure — always delivered with the same rigor and attention to detail that has defined us from day one.
